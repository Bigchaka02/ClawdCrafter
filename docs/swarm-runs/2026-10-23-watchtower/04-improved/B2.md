# B2 (revised): medieval stone watchtower with a spruce roof

Size: W = 16 (x), H = 16 (y), D = 16 (z). Coordinates: x west to east, y ground (0) upward, z north (0) to south (15). Front and entrance face south.

Source: the final design brief (sections 0, 13 and Appendix A), revised after two critiques. The revisions: the entrance path is one block wide, in line with the door; a corbel course of stone bricks at y=9 now carries the roof eave; two standing lanterns were added (ground room and upper room). The array is generated by `04-improved/scripts/build_b2.py`, written to `B2_array.json`, re-read from that file and checked cell by cell, with the checks listed at the end.

Totals: 4096 cells, 1284 non-air, 2812 air (`.`). Air in the ground room (x and z 5..10, y 1..4): 133. Air in the upper room (x and z 5..10, y 6..9): 135.

## Openings and interior details

- Entrance door (south wall, outer cell z = 12, x = 7): lower half (7,1,12) and upper half (7,2,12), `oak_door`, facing=north, hinge=left. The passage (7,1,11) and (7,2,11) is air. Threshold (7,0,12) is mossy cobblestone. The path (7,0,13), (7,0,14), (7,0,15) runs straight out from the door to the south edge.
- Arrow loops, one wide and two tall, at y = 2..3, through both wall cells: west z = 9 (x 3..4), east z = 9 (x 11..12), north x = 5 (z 3..4), south x = 10 (z 11..12). 16 air cells.
- Windows, upper storey y = 7..8, one wide and two tall through both wall cells: north x = 8 (z 3..4), south x = 8 (z 11..12), west z = 8 (x 3..4), east z = 8 (x 11..12). 16 glass panes with connection states from their neighbours.
- Corbel course: stone bricks at y = 9 on the perimeter of x and z 2..13 (44 cells). It sits under the eave, one block past the walls, so every eave stair has support.
- Ladder: `ladder[facing=east]` at x = 5, z = 7, y = 1..6, attached to the wall at x = 4. The deck hole is (5,5,7).
- Ground room: barrels (9,1,5), (10,1,5); chest (10,1,6); crafting table (9,1,10); furnace (6,1,5); hanging lantern (8,4,8); standing lantern (10,1,8). The aisle is kept clear at x = 7, z = 6..11 and at (6,1,7).
- Upper room: red bed foot (6,6,9), head (7,6,9), facing=east; bell (9,6,6) on the deck; chest (10,6,10); bookshelves (5,6,9), (5,6,10); standing lantern (6,6,6); hanging lantern (8,9,8).
- Light: six lanterns. Hanging at (8,4,8) and (8,9,8); standing at (6,1,13) and (8,1,13) either side of the path, (10,1,8) in the ground room and (6,6,6) in the upper room.
- Weathering: mossy band at y = 1 on the outer shell (31 cells); six cracked cells at (6,3,3), (12,3,6), (3,7,6), (11,9,12), (4,4,12), (12,6,11).
- Exterior: grass yard (153 cells), dirt path (7,0,13..15), four poppies at (1,1,1), (14,1,1), (1,1,14), (14,1,14).
- Roof: six layers at y = 10..15. Layers y = 10..14 are spruce stair rings (120 stairs, all `half=bottom`, `shape=straight`) with spruce planks at the corners and inside. The apex is 2 x 2 spruce planks at y = 15. The roof is mirror-symmetric about x = 7.5 and z = 7.5.

## Design notes

- The door is at x = 7, half a block left of the centre of the 10-block wall (the centre is between x = 7 and x = 8). An even-width wall has no centre block, so the door stays at x = 7. The path is one block wide on the same column, so the door and the path line up, and the two lanterns at the door stand at x = 6 and x = 8, either side of it.
- The roof corners are full spruce planks, not stairs. A stair with an outer-corner shape would depend on its neighbours, and its direction cannot be checked from the render, so the corners stay as planks.
- The roof reads as stepped blocks because every block is drawn as a cube. The apex is the highest block, at y = 15.
- The tower has no battlements or gallery, as in the brief (decision 0.2). A battlement at y = 9 would collide with the corbel course and the eave.
