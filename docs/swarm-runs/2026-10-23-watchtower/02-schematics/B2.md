# B2: medieval stone watchtower with a spruce roof

Size: W = 16 (x), H = 16 (y), D = 16 (z). Coordinates: x west to east, y ground (0) upward, z north (0) to south (15). Front and entrance face south.

Source: the final design brief, section 13 (data) and Appendix A (generator). The array was extracted from the brief, checked with the brief's 29 generator checks, and matched cell by cell to the generator's cell map.

Totals: 4096 cells, 1238 non-air, 2858 air (`.`).

## Openings and interior details

- Entrance door (south wall, outer cell z = 12, x = 7): lower half (7,1,12) and upper half (7,2,12), `oak_door`, facing=north, hinge=left. The passage (7,1,11) and (7,2,11) is air. Threshold (7,0,12) is mossy cobblestone.
- Arrow loops, one wide and two tall, at y = 2..3, through both wall cells: west z = 9 (x 3..4), east z = 9 (x 11..12), north x = 5 (z 3..4), south x = 10 (z 11..12). 16 air cells.
- Windows, upper storey y = 7..8, one wide and two tall through both wall cells: north x = 8 (z 3..4), south x = 8 (z 11..12), west z = 8 (x 3..4), east z = 8 (x 11..12). 16 glass panes with connection states from their neighbours.
- Ladder: `ladder[facing=east]` at x = 5, z = 7, y = 1..6, attached to the wall at x = 4. The deck hole is (5,5,7).
- Ground room: barrels (9,1,5), (10,1,5); chest (10,1,6); crafting table (9,1,10); furnace (6,1,5); hanging lantern (8,4,8). Aisle kept clear at x = 7 and z = 6..11.
- Upper room: red bed foot (6,6,9), head (7,6,9), facing=east; bell (9,6,6) on the deck; chest (10,6,10); bookshelves (5,6,9), (5,6,10); hanging lantern (8,9,8).
- Light: lanterns only. Hanging at (8,4,8) and (8,9,8); standing at (6,1,13) and (9,1,13), either side of the path.
- Weathering: mossy band at y = 1 on the outer shell (31 cells); six cracked cells at (6,3,3), (12,3,6), (3,7,6), (11,9,12), (4,4,12), (12,6,11).
- Exterior: grass yard (150 cells), dirt path x 7..8, z 13..15, four poppies at (1,1,1), (14,1,1), (1,1,14), (14,1,14).
- Roof: six layers at y = 10..15. Layers y = 10..14 are spruce stair rings (120 stairs, all `half=bottom`, `shape=straight`) with spruce planks at the corners and inside. The apex is 2 x 2 spruce planks at y = 15.
