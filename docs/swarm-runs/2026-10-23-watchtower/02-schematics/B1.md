# B1 schematic: medieval stone watchtower with spruce roof

Size: W = 16 (x), H = 16 (y), D = 16 (z). Coordinates: x 0 = west to 15 = east; y 0 = ground to 15 = top; z 0 = north to 15 = south. Front and main entrance: the south face (z = 12 outer face), door at x = 7. The tower is 10 x 10 at x and z 3..12, with a 12 x 12 roof eave at x and z 2..13.

Layers are character maps: 16 rows (z = 0 north first), 16 characters each (x = 0 west first). `.` is air. Identical consecutive layers are written once.

Totals: 2858 air cells and 1238 non-air cells (4096 in all).
